# PPR Frontier textures

Seven original 64 by 64 RGBA repeating textures were generated for PPR from integer grain and periodic trigonometric patterns. No third-party or extracted game art was used. The PNGs carry an sRGB chunk. Masonry repeats once per metre and has two staggered courses. The other textures repeat every two metres. Material constants are game tuning, not measured physical data.

Five additional original 64 by 64 RGBA albedo tiles (clay, limestone, salt, sulfur, crystal) use periodic sediment bands, cubic grain, mineral mottling and triangular facets, with deterministic integer grain. These grayscale sRGB tiles take their color from the material tint. No external assets or libraries were added. Clay/limestone repeat every two metres; salt/sulfur/crystal every metre. Material values remain game tuning.
