# ppr.stylized — generated asset pack

Generated by the diffusion tileset pipeline in `/home/bek/Projects/textures` (`ppr_export_stylized.py`), following the artistic direction of `PPR_env.png`, `PPR_env2.jpg` and `PPR_env3.jpg`.

- Terrain albedo/normal: SDXL-Turbo img2img family anchors (7 canonical + 4 sheet-3 anchor families: muck, refined_carbon, rusted_ore, halite) retinted to each material's target colour, seam-blended, de-lit (low-frequency illumination removed and luminance detail compressed to base colour; lighting is reconstructed in realtime from the normal/AO/roughness channels), downscaled to 256x256. Structural technique variety is applied pre-tint per material (grit / shatter / flakes / bubbles / billows / glint; pebble is the base family look) with per-material parameters and a toroidal domain warp so no two materials share an anchor pattern; all wrap-safe.
- Wear & tear is procedural-first (deterministic scratches, pits, dust patina, edge chips per material) and only afterwards a GPU img2img augmentation pass naturalizes the synthetic layers into final detail. The pass tries 2 seeds per material and keeps the highest micro-detail candidate, then restores the authored luminance percentiles, hue/saturation (small drift budget) and half of the authored mid/high-frequency relief, so diffusion can add natural structure without recolouring or flattening the tile. A stable (name + colour + version) cache key makes --no-gpu re-exports bit-reproducible. The shipped albedo is then de-lit and contrast-flattened so it holds no baked illumination; normals and the other PBR sidecars are still derived from the detailed pre-flatten source.
- Ore/crystal identity pass (post-augment, pre-downscale): iconic chunky crystal shards are composited into metal ores and crystalline minerals — uranium and autunite vivid green, copper verdigris + native copper, iron rust-orange, wolframite cold steel-blue, and gem-grade flat facet planes with sharp seams for the coloured crystal family — so materials read at a glance even where that is less geologically literal. Shard/facet relief feeds the derived normal/height/AO channels; `_opacity` stays keyed to the pre-overlay albedo.
- backgrounds/: diffusion img2img anchored on the default pack layers, default alpha stencil preserved.
- PBR sidecar channels (not registered in pack.toml — the manifest is near its 64 KiB cap), 10 per terrain material: `_ao` / `_cavity` / `_fog` / `_height` / `_roughness` (material TOML roughness plus a per-material variation profile: gems stay glossy, metals micro-vary, dry terrain stays matte, wet moss sheens) / `_transmittance` / `_wetness` (all height-derived from one shared field, so AO matches the shipped height), `_material_id` (a unique stable identity colour per material), `_emissive` (color glow) and `_opacity` — the authored tile border in the marching-squares top-edge language (interior opaque, four-edge chunky pebble-modulated hard iso-contour with a ~1 px anti-aliased transition, per ref_crops s1/s3_ms_top_edge). Each PNG ships a ready `asset-texture_.._png.toml` descriptor in the same folder; register later with two manifest lines each when the engine learns to consume them.
- Authored masonry, refined copper/iron/aluminum and glass preserve their default source structure, without the rock-variation, wear or augmentation passes. Pigment tinting preserves manufactured surfaces.
- Ore roughness and emission follow the source mineral footprint: glossy/emissive veins over matte non-emitting host rock. The offline preview uses GGX/Smith/Schlick reflectance. Runtime scalar roughness is the map average; regional sidecars remain unregistered.
- showcase props: relit default pixels (structure-critical).
- All TOML descriptors cloned from the default pack; only `pack.toml pack_id` and material `[appearance].color_rgba` (texture means) and `[appearance].roughness` (map averages) were edited.

Reproduce with `.venv/bin/python ppr_export_stylized.py`.
