## Follow-up findings and resolutions

1. **Execution permission:** the initial report called Task0's checkpoint scope
   “already authorized.” The lane explicitly distinguished that future commit's
   scope from permission to begin: “No — an implementer may not start yet.” It
   requires owner plan-review confirmation before Task0.
2. **Scheduling concern withdrawn:** “2×2 group” means four **probes**, not cells
   or GPU threads. It computed L1:65,536 probes/4=16,384 selected, each16 directions;
   L5:256/4=64 selected, each4,096 directions. Both update262,144 directional
   intervals per level per scene frame. It retracted its ambiguity as resolved
   by the plan's four-frame cycle and separately defined probe spacing.
3. **Recovery archive scope:** mandatory external preflight records/binary diffs
   and verified checkpoint extraction were correctly separated from the optional
   extra copied-source archive. No source archive may be committed to Git.
4. **Two-dimensional extents:** the initial calculation emphasized width but quoted
   the full example. Follow-up gave all axes explicitly: R961×541, S481×271,
   B962×542 for1921×1081,P2.
5. **Linked-design dependency list:** the lane corrected omissions of Tasks2 and16
   from its final list. Post formulas and inspection-view details still require
   §7; this does not mean Task8's fully specified visibility construction is absent.
6. **Submission state distinctions:** pending completion is ordinary known in-flight
   work, not ambiguous submit fate. Known rejection permits discard of the rejected
   attempt's unsubmitted work; known acceptance pins resources through its fence;
   unknown fate permits neither normal commit nor discard and stops admission.
7. **Discard precision:** follow-up expressly prohibited rejected-B cleanup from
   destroying A's scene/world data, old accepted history or any resource still
   held by earlier reader fences. Only unsubmitted work/tickets may be released.
8. **Dispatch precision:** both entries use256 threads/group. Visibility uses32×32
   groups and four cells/thread; jump uses64×64 groups and one cell/thread.
   “One thread per cell” never means one thread per group.

## Quantitative and semantic checks

- L1:65,536 probes×16 directions; L5:256 probes×4,096 directions. Each has
  1,048,576 eight-byte records=8MiB; all five coarse levels=40MiB. Fine CH=8MiB;
  two winner grids=8MiB. Ray starts are native-cell distances, not allocation offsets.
- Visibility records=21,504×8=172,032bytes; separate shared masks=128+32=160bytes.
  Every mask has one writer, every native cell is rebuilt, and maximality is checked
  independently rather than accepting merely conservative undersized jumps.
- Motion in resolved pixels excludes jitter: motion0→current weight0.05,
  motion0.3→0.15, motion2→0.25; invalid history is current-only.
- Finite clear segment: RGB0/T1; opaque black: RGB0/T0; black terminal sky: RGB0/T0;
  lit terminal sky: RGB sky-sector mean/T0. Terminal closure is not a hit coordinate.
- Source reads previous CH before in-place fine writes; visible/source thermal
  sampling is shared and6500K maps to luminance1. Pure metals retain visible PBR
  while diffuse feedback is zero; receive-only/gas do not become transport sources.
- Task19 needs both `clang-dev` and `clang-rel` clean builds with zero warnings/errors,
  complete normal-execution logs and an all-enabled-severity flooding audit.
  Deliberate fault-test logs remain separately identified evidence, not exemptions
  for normal runs.
